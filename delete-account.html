Delete Your Vyaya Account

Last updated: June 6, 2025

1. Overview

Vyaya lets you permanently delete your account and all associated data directly within the app. Follow the steps below.

2. How to Delete Your Account in the App

1. Open the Vyaya app and sign in. 2. Go to Settings. 3. Scroll to the Account section and tap Delete Account. 4. Read the warning, tap Continue, then type your email address to confirm. 5. Tap Delete Account Permanently. Your account and all data are immediately and permanently deleted.

3. What Data Is Deleted

When you delete your account, the following are permanently removed from our systems: • Your account and profile (name, email) • All transactions, wallets, budgets, debts, and recurring entries • All categories you created • Your AI usage history This deletion is immediate and cannot be undone. No data is retained after deletion.

4. Alternative Request Method

If you are unable to access the app, you can request account deletion by emailing support.vyaya@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account. We will process your request and confirm deletion.
